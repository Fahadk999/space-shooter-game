> An action-packed 2D arcade shooter built with **Python** and **Pygame-CE**. Upgrade your stats, survive waves of increasingly tough enemies, and beat your high score!

---

## Features

* **Dynamic Upgrades:** Purchase upgrades for Health, Reload Speed, Bullet Damage, and Health Regeneration in real time.
* **Enemy Scaling:** Waves become faster and stronger over time.
* **Persistent High Scores:** Saves your best runs locally using SQLite.
* **Sound Effects & UI Glows:** Polished feedback for player actions and hits.

---

## How to Play

### Controls
* **Movement:** `WASD` or `Arrow Keys`
* **Upgrades:** Press keys `1`–`5` or click the in-game `+` buttons.
